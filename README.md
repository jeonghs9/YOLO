# Vision Research

Computer vision research workspace organized by task rather than by a single model family.

## Research Areas

- [`vision-det/`](./vision-det/README.md): object detection research
- [`vision-seg/`](./vision-seg/README.md): segmentation research
- [`docs/`](./docs/): workspace guides and cleanup records

Large datasets, experiment runs, W&B artifacts, checkpoints, and Conda environments are managed outside Git.
